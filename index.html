About Us

Scent, managed the way your space deserves.

AERVESSA is a commercial scenting studio. We build custom fragrance profiles for hotels, offices, retail stores, studios, and hospitality spaces, then install and service the diffusion equipment ourselves — so you never have to think about it again.

We're a small, hands-on team, which means every account gets a fragrance chosen for its space, a maintenance schedule that's actually kept, and a person who picks up the phone when you call.

  • Custom fragrance profile matched to your brand and floor plan
  • Professional-grade diffusion equipment, installed and calibrated on site
  • Scheduled servicing — refills, cleaning, and adjustments included
